🐔

Poultry Record Digitizer

A college project that converts handwritten or printed poultry farm register photos into structured digital data using OCR technology. It extracts ABW, FCR, medicine notes and more — automatically.

📖 How to Use

1

Login or Register

Create a free account and sign in to access the dashboard.

2

Upload or Capture Image

Upload a gallery photo or use your phone camera to capture your register sheet directly.

3

Wait for Processing

The system compresses the image, runs dual OCR (EasyOCR + Tesseract), and extracts structured data.

4

Explore the Dashboard

After processing, the dashboard shows your summary. Navigate to Report, Alerts, or Insights.

5

Edit & Export

On the Report page, edit any incorrect values and download as Excel or PDF.

📷 Tips for Best OCR Results

  • ☀️ Use natural light or bright indoor lighting — avoid dark or shadowy conditions
  • 📐 Hold the camera flat and parallel to the paper — avoid angles
  • 🔍 Ensure the entire register page fits in the frame
  • 📱 Use the highest camera resolution available on your device
  • 🚫 Avoid blurry or out-of-focus images — retake if needed
  • 📄 Printed or typed registers give better accuracy than handwritten
  • 🛠️ Check the Debug section on the Report page if results seem wrong
  • 🖼️ Open processed_debug.jpg to inspect the preprocessed image
